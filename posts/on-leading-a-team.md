1. I believe that my primary goal when leading a team is to optimise for my team's learning. If I have optimised for my team's learning, anything else (delivering a product) is a side effect.
2. Regular interactions with my team serve as the vehicle for driving the growth of the engineers under my care. These regular interactions happen in weekly 1:1s, code reviews, architectural discussions, watercooler conversations and whatnot.
3. The foundation for growth of the engineers under my care is psychological safety. Psychological safety is a result of making sure that an engineer has their personal life sorted, a comfortable working environment, feels included in the team, has some downtime for reflection, and an avenue to vent their frustrations.
4. The only thing I can ask of an engineer under my care is to strive to do good work.
5. The marker for growth in an engineer is an increase in their scope of work. For example, an engineer starts out working on a small component in a service and eventually graduates to working on an entire module in a service.
6. It is very important that the team celebrates victories often.
7. A good whiteboard and a set of nice whiteboard markers is one of the best investments you can make for a team.
